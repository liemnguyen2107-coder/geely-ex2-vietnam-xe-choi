The list below comes straight from the app's feature catalog (`PaidFeatureCatalog`), version **1.3.2**.

### 🚘 Vehicle control

- Lock the doors, see which doors are open or closed
- Open / close the front and rear trunk
- Lower, raise or crack open each window, or all four at once
- Drive mode **Eco / Comfort / Sport**, remembered after the car is turned off
- Regenerative braking **low / medium / high**, remembered after the car is turned off
- Climate: temperature, fan, airflow direction, recirculation / fresh air, defrost
- Digital temperature shown on the car's climate bar
- Tyre pressure (TPMS) display
- Mute and change the pedestrian warning sound (AVAS)
- Car wash mode, app cleaner, auto-launch apps at start-up

### 🔋 Energy and trips

- Battery, mileage, range, charging status and charge limit
- **Trips:** km, kWh, GPS route on the map, viewable by 7 days / 30 days / 3 months
- **Charge journal:** records kWh, AC/DC and electricity cost for every charge
- **Nearby chargers:** finds chargers within 20 km (EV HUB), filterable by the networks you have topped up with
- **Battery %** shown on the status bar next to the Wi‑Fi icon

### 🎙️ Voice commands

- Commands in **Vietnamese and English** via Google (needs Wi‑Fi and Google services), understands natural sentences, fast response
- Start with the **voice button on the steering wheel**; a sound wave on the bar and a "ting" tell you it is listening
- **Steering-wheel arrow keys:** assign next track, drive mode, regen braking, fan (track skipping also works in YouTube / YT Music)

### 🖥️ Interface and display

- **Split screen** that keeps the car's status bar
- **Floating widget:** show on the Home screen or over every app
- **3 quick-launch slots**, long-press to change the app
- Wi‑Fi and split-screen icons on the status bar
- Screen brightness for day and night
- Auto-dim the screen when idle while music keeps playing
- **Light / dark** interface following the car's day-night mode
- **Mirror camera on turns:** shows the left / right side camera according to the turn signal, rear wheel area only
- Dark-background reverse camera, less glare at night *(Pro version)*

### 📱 Phone connection

- Send **photos** from your phone to the car's gallery (JPG, PNG, HEIF) via QR code
- Send a **Google Maps link** from your phone and the car navigates to the place
- 📲 **Control the car via Telegram:** ask for battery, turn on the A/C, lock the doors, find the car, send a destination, get charging alerts and "you left the car on" reminders. [Read the full guide →](TELEGRAM.md)

### 🧩 App store and system

- **App Store** to install apps directly on the car, with a Vietnamese voice guide
- Hide helper apps from the car's app drawer; keep EX2 VN Control, Trips, microG, entertainment and navigation apps
- Auto-connect to Wi‑Fi
- Close apps when the screen locks to save resources
- Keep apps from being killed by the car when they go to the background
- **OTA:** receive new versions directly on the car
